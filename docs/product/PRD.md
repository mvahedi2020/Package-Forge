# Package Forge — requirements

## Decision and route

Choose a fictional team → review applied assumptions → compare three packages → inspect no-fit → review a versioned renewal quote → cancel or confirm a local simulation. A buyer must be able to explain why the package meets every requirement, what it costs, what is lost and when it takes effect.

## Fit and cost

A fitting tier must contain all must-haves and both role capacities. Lowest-cost fit is provisional for the applied assumptions, never a claim of customer preference. A no-fit scenario blocks all quote confirmation. Changing needs requires a separate review; silently removing approvals to recommend Base is prohibited. Free viewers cost $0 and never enter paid-editor math. The hypothetical catalog has no minimum seat fee; zero editors yields $0 even on a paid package if capacities and capabilities fit. This is a sample rule, not real vendor pricing.

Monthly charge = editors × monthly editor price. Annual charge = editors × annual equivalent editor price × 12. Annual equivalent per month is labeled comparison-only. No taxes, proration or refunds are calculated. Whole counts are 0–10,000; values above tier capacity are valid assumptions but no-fit. Negative, decimal, blank and nonfinite counts block applying assumptions.

## State and recovery

Active baseline is immutable. A confirmation adds an immutable quote and schedules it for next renewal; replacement retains old history. Quote version, catalog, baseline version, exact assumptions/revision, fixed sample date, expiration, charge and term dates must agree. Every confirm checks current selection, draft, revision and full saved content. Unsupported or inconsistent saved data is preserved until deliberate reset. External corruption remains recoverable through an always-accessible reset preview.

Undo restores exactly one previous applied assumptions draft until refresh or a commitment action, clears selection and creates a new revision. It never changes active or scheduled plans/history. Cancel-preview changes no commitment. Cancel-scheduled retains quotes and active baseline, does not revive older schedules and cannot be undone. Full sample reset clears draft, selection, history, scheduled changes and Undo, retaining the original active baseline; reset requires confirmation and cannot be undone.
