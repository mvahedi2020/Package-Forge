# Package Forge — product brief

A fictional team buyer needs to choose a package without losing a must-have capability or mistaking an annual equivalent for an actual monthly charge. The observable outcome is a fitting package, a traceable renewal amount, and an understood downgrade consequence before a local commitment.

Mo owns product and program direction. AI assisted implementation and verification. The product choices below are prototype recommendations; no owner endorsement, customer research, willingness-to-pay result or commercial outcome is claimed.

The central decision is to put requirements before price. A price-only table is faster to scan but can encourage a cheaper tier that omits approvals. A recommendation wizard is simpler but can hide the assumptions behind the answer. This worksheet exposes fit failures, paid versus free roles, arithmetic and next-renewal consequences in one route.

Original samples: Cedar Design, Kite Collective, Harbor Records and Widefield Network. Three hypothetical packages use illustrative USD prices. Scenarios describe the proposed team at renewal; the active fictional Studio baseline stays fixed. No payment processor, tax engine, refund, proration, authentication, external integration, live AI or price optimization.

Deep teal signals the worksheet, mint marks fitting choices and coral signals a decision or exception. The design emphasizes a comparison sheet instead of a metrics dashboard.
